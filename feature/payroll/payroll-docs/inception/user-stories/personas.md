# User Personas: Payroll Module

## 1. Admin (Family Business Owner/Manager)
- **Role**: Manages the overall business operations, including finances and workforce management across multiple business lines (Mill, Harvester, Ploughing).
- **Goals**:
    - Accurately calculate weekly payroll based on business income percentages.
    - Track employee loans (cash and products) to ensure recovery.
    - Maintain a digital audit trail of all financial transactions and attendance.
    - Provide physical payslips to employees for transparency.
- **Pain Points**:
    - Manual calculation errors in percentage-based salaries.
    - Forgetting to deduct loans or tracking product price changes.
    - Managing physical receipts for business expenses.

## 2. Worker (Mill & Logistics / Harvester / Ploughing)
- **Role**: Performs operational tasks (milling, delivery, harvesting, or ploughing).
- **Goals**:
    - Ensure attendance is recorded accurately.
    - Receive fair payment based on the agreed business percentage.
    - Get clear documentation (payslip) of earnings and deductions.
    - Access loans (cash or products like rice) when needed.
- **Pain Points**:
    - Uncertainty about how salary was calculated.
    - No clear record of remaining loan balances.
